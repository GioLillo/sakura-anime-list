# 🌸 Sakura Anime List

**A quiet, personal home for your anime collection.**

Sakura Shelf is a minimal anime library with a dark interface, soft lavender accents, sakura details, and a small anime character in the sidebar. It is designed to keep the focus on the anime, not on unnecessary features.

## 🚀 Live Demo

Try the live app here: [https://giolillo.github.io/sakura-anime-list/](https://giolillo.github.io/sakura-anime-list/)

## ✨ What you can do

- **Search anime** using public anime catalogues.
- **Preview the cover** before adding a title to your collection.
- **Rate anime** from 1 to 10.
- **Browse your library** by title or category.
- **View simple statistics**, including your average rating and most common categories.
- **Keep your collection synced** across devices by signing in to the same account.
- **Change your username or password** from your account page.


## 👷🏻‍♀️ How it works

1. Create an account or sign in.
2. Open **Add Anime** and search for a title.
3. Select the correct result to preview its cover and automatically detected category.
4. Choose your rating and add it to your library.
5. Visit **Library** or **Statistics** to explore your collection.

Sakura Shelf uses public anime catalogues (Jikan, Kitsu, and AniList) to look up titles, covers, and genre information. Search availability and metadata can vary when an external catalogue is temporarily unavailable or does not provide a particular field.

## 🛡️ Privacy

Your library is associated with your signed-in account and is not intended to be a public social profile. The hosted version uses Supabase for authentication and data storage. Database access must be protected by the configured row-level security policies.

## 🛠️ Technology

- HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- Supabase Authentication and database
- Jikan, Kitsu, and AniList for anime metadata
- Progressive Web App support

Sakura Shelf is a personal project built to make keeping track of watched anime simple, calm, and enjoyable. 🌸
